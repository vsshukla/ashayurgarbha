Welcome to Asha AyurGarha

Traditional Ayurveda, with care that starts by listening.

At Asha AyurGarha, we welcome individuals and families seeking Ayurvedic guidance for health and wellbeing. Our approach begins with understanding your concerns, health history and daily routine, then discussing care options suited to you.

  • ✓One-to-one consultation in a respectful, comfortable setting.

How we may help

Treatments & consultation areas

Explore the concerns for which patients may seek an appointment. A doctor will assess suitability and explain available options.

🌸

Women’s Wellness

Supportive consultation for reproductive and menstrual health concerns.

  • PCOS / PCOD
  • Irregular or heavy periods
  • Ovarian cysts & fibroids
  • Endometriosis-related concerns
  • Vaginal discharge or pelvic discomfort
🌿

Men’s Wellness

Confidential consultation for men’s reproductive and sexual health concerns.

  • Fertility-related concerns
  • Low sperm count
  • Erectile difficulties
  • Low libido
  • Other intimate health concerns
🧘

Joint & Mobility Care

Assessment and supportive care discussions for musculoskeletal concerns.

  • Arthritis and joint pain
  • Sciatica-related pain
  • Back and body discomfort
  • Mobility and daily function
🍃

Skin & Hair Concerns

Ayurvedic consultation for selected skin and hair concerns.

  • Common skin conditions
  • Hair and scalp concerns
  • Personalized lifestyle guidance
☀️

Headache & Wellbeing

Consultation for recurring discomfort and general wellness needs.

  • Migraine and headaches
  • Sleep and stress-related concerns
  • General wellness guidance
🪷

Personalized Ayurveda

Discuss traditional Ayurvedic approaches for other health concerns.

  • Individual assessment
  • Diet and daily-routine guidance
  • Follow-up care planning

The conditions listed are areas for consultation, not a promise of diagnosis, cure or outcome. Treatment is recommended only after an appropriate clinical assessment.

Helpful to know

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an appointment?

We recommend contacting the clinic first so the team can confirm availability and guide you.

Will a treatment be suggested immediately?

The doctor will first understand your concern and assess whether an Ayurvedic approach is appropriate.

Can I continue my current medicines?

Do not stop or change prescribed medicines without speaking to the clinician who prescribed them. Share your current medicines during consultation.

What if I have urgent symptoms?

For severe, sudden or emergency symptoms, seek urgent medical care rather than waiting for an appointment.
